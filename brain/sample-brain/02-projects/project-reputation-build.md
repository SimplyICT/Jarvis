# Project — reputation build for PaintCo

**Client:** [[client-mike-johnson]]
**Value:** $2,500 ex GST, fixed price
**Status:** delivered, ready to invoice

## What was delivered

1. Google Business Profile cleanup and category correction
2. Review-request automation wired into their job-completion flow
3. Two landing pages targeting "painter [suburb]" long-tail searches
4. A monthly reputation report template

## Deliverable notes

The review-request automation is the part Mike was most excited about. It sends
an SMS two hours after a job is marked complete, with a direct review link.
Early result: review velocity went from ~2/month to ~9/month.
