# SimplyICT — business profile

SimplyICT is an Australian managed IT and cyber-security provider. We sell
**managed services** on a per-seat monthly retainer, plus project work.

## Positioning

We are not the cheapest. We win on response time and on the fact that the
client gets a named engineer, not a queue. The pitch that lands is
"same-day response, fixed monthly price, no surprise invoices".

## Ideal client

- 10–80 seats
- Already has some compliance pressure (Essential Eight, ISO 27001, cyber insurance)
- Has been burned by a break-fix provider

## Brand voice

Plain English. No jargon. Confident but never arrogant. Australian spelling.
Never promise an uptime number we have not contractually signed.
