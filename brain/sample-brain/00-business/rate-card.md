# Rate card and invoicing rules

## Hourly rates

| Service | Rate (ex GST) |
|---|---|
| Standard remote support | $165 / hour |
| After-hours support | $240 / hour (rising to **$260 / hour** from 1 Oct 2026) |
| Onsite attendance | $220 / hour + travel |
| Project engineering | $190 / hour |
| Security assessment | $2,400 / day |

## Retainers

Managed services are billed monthly in advance per seat. Standard seat is
**$95 per seat per month**. Add-ons:

- Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR): +$12 / seat
- Backup & disaster recovery: +$18 / seat
- Security awareness training: +$4 / seat

## Invoicing rules

1. Invoices go out on the **1st** of the month for retainers.
2. Project work is invoiced **on completion**, or milestone-based for anything over $10,000.
3. Payment terms are **14 days**. Late accounts get a friendly call before any formal notice.
4. Always quote the client's PO number if they have one.
5. Never discount more than 10% without the director's approval.

## Rate changes

- **After-hours support:** rises from $240 to **$260 / hour** ex GST, effective
  **1 October 2026**. The $240 rate applies to work up to and including
  30 September 2026.

## Minimum engagement

Nothing under **$495** ex GST. If a job is smaller than that, roll it into the
next retainer invoice.
